# COOPCONNECT-
CoopConnect creates a fair, transparent, and AI-driven employment ecosystem that helps skilled cooperative workers get more opportunities while helping customers and contractors quickly find the right workforce.
👷 Verified worker registration and skill profiling
🤖 AI-assisted team formation and worker matching
📅 Worker availability calendar to prevent double booking
⚖️ Fair job distribution based on workload and cooperative capacity
💰 Digital agreements and milestone/weekly payments
⭐ Two-way ratings and transparent work records
🔄 AI-based replacement suggestions when workers become unavailable
📊 Demand forecasting for future workforce requirements
